DiscTracker Housing

The pod twists into a dock glued under the flight plate, a 30° turn that clicks as it tightens. Three lugs sit over a solid lip, so a hard landing can't pull it out, and the clicks stop it unscrewing. Every part prints flat on a Bambu P1S in PETG with no supports. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom.

Fits and gaps

Each fit is checked at nominal, statistically (RSS, the normal way to qualify a printed part), and at the absolute worst case of every tolerance stacking the same way. Printed features are taken as ±0.1 mm, which a calibrated P1S holds in XY; the RSS check is repeated at ±0.2 mm in report.json. Height errors don't loosen the lock: they only change how far the pod turns before it's tight.

How it works

Twist in

Hold the pod with its USB-C port at 11 o'clock to the gap in the dock. Push it up and the three lugs drop through the dock's notches. The wide lug only fits one notch, so it goes in one way. Turn it clockwise to 12 o'clock. It clicks every 3° as the lugs ride up ramps on the dock's lip, then stops: the lid is tight against the disc.

Stays in

The lugs sit over a solid lip, 1.9 mm deep, so nothing has to flex to hold the pod. A landing would have to break the lugs or the lip. Three spring pawls in the dock click over short racks on the pod and stop it unscrewing when a tree hit tries to spin it loose.

Take out

Grip the ribs below the dock and turn it back about 30°, counterclockwise from below. It clicks out more stiffly than it went in, then drops free.

Lights, sound, reset

The slot over the XIAO's lights has a shroud, so the red charge light and the RGB status light show up sharp. The buzzer sits in a sealed chamber with two 1.6 mm holes, tuned near its 4 kHz tone. Reset is a pinhole: press the switch with a paperclip.

Files

Printing (Bambu P1S)

PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, no supports, 4 walls and 100 % infill. Pod lid-down, dock with the tape face up, cover outer face down. Print the dock at 0.12 mm layers so the ramps are smooth. Print test_ring and one dock first and check the twist.

SolidWorks

Open the .step files in hardware/cad/out/: pod, cover, dock, test_ring, jig_frame, jig_plunger, plus housing_assembly.step with the board inside.

Changing it

Every size is a parameter at the top of hardware/cad/housing.py. If the test print turns too stiffly or too loosely, rebuild with --fit-adjust 0.05 or -0.05. --process mjf builds the version for ordering in PA11 from JLC3DP.
