DiscTracker Housing

The pod clicks into a dock glued under the flight plate. To remove it, squeeze both latches through the dock's windows and pull. The cover has a reset button that can't be over-pressed, a window for the XIAO's charge and status lights, and a tuned sound port for the buzzer. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom.

How it works

Click in

The flat by the USB-C port lines up with a flat in the dock, so the pod only goes in one way. Push it up. Four spring fingers in the dock snap over the ridge at the top of the pod and pull it flat against the disc with no rattle. At the same moment, both latches snap into the dock's windows.

Take out

Press both latch pads through the dock windows with thumb and finger, then pull. The latch hooks are square, so a hard landing can't pull the pod out. The springs alone would let go at about 14 N.

Reset button

Press the ring on the cover. A flexible tongue pushes a small spring arm onto the XIAO's reset switch at about 3.8 N. A strip on the tongue then lands on the USB-C shell, so pressing harder never reaches the switch.

Lights and sound

The slot over the XIAO's lights has a shroud, so the red charge light and the RGB status light show up sharp. The buzzer sits in a sealed chamber with two 1.6 mm holes, tuned near its 4 kHz tone.

Files

SolidWorks

Open the .step files in hardware/cad/out/: pod, cover, dock, jig_frame, jig_plunger, plus housing_assembly.step with the board inside.

Ordering

Order the .stl files from JLC3DP in PA11-HP (MJF). The flexures need its 50 % elongation. Print the jig in PETG or PLA at home. You also need three M2 × 6 countersunk screws in nylon or brass, nothing steel near the magnetometer.

Changing it

Every size is a parameter at the top of hardware/cad/housing.py. Re-run it to rebuild the files and report.json, which lists every fit shown here.
