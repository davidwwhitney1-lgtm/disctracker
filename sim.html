    DiscTracker Sim

    Every throw flies on this screen automatically.

    1. Wake the disc (pick it up or give it a shake). Close the phone app if it's connected.
    2. Click Connect and choose DiscTracker.
    3. Throw. Stand at least about 3 m (10 ft) from the net so the disc flies freely for a moment – spin and wobble are measured in that free flight.
