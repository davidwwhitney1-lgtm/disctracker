Calibrate
  1. Mount the board in the center of the disc, under the flight plate, lying flat.
  2. Lay the disc flat, top side up, on a table or the floor.
  3. Tap Calibrate and don't touch the disc for 3 seconds.
Connect to the disc first.

Do this once after mounting (and again if the board moves). It's saved on the disc, so turning it off doesn't lose it.

Roll/pitch are only accurate while the disc is still. Spin above ~333 rpm exceeds the gyro's range; the board then estimates it from the accelerometer.
