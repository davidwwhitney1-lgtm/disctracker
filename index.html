Power
–
Battery: –
  • Standby – saves most of the battery. Tap Wake up, or just pick up the disc. Happens on its own after 5 min without motion.
  • Turn off – saves the most. While off the disc can't hear Bluetooth, so turn it back on with a firm flick or shake. It also turns itself off after 4 h in standby with no phone, or when the battery is low. (Never while plugged into USB – it uses standby then.)

Version 2026-10-07c. Roll/pitch are only accurate while the disc is still. Spin above ~333 rpm exceeds the gyro's range; the board then estimates it from the accelerometer.
